# Changes

## 1.2

- Pills in the line are tighter: smaller margins and dot, a 13 sp face.
  At four cells wide a label now has room for five or six letters; in 1.1
  even four were cut.
- The project page sits next to the APK, with a live line to try.

## 1.1

- A single line of glass by default, four cells wide: four pills and a
  round edit button, in the look of a glass card.
- Stretch it wider and more pills fit in the line, up to six. Stretch it
  taller and the pills go into two rows of three, then three rows of two.
- The glass can be dimmed in four steps, 10 to 90 percent, for each widget.
- The interface is English only.

## 1.0

The first build.

- Six pills with phrases. A tap puts the phrase on the clipboard without
  opening any window, and the pill floods with its own colour for a moment.
- Each pill keeps its colour by place.
- An empty pill opens the editor on its slot.
- Every widget on the home screen keeps its own six phrases.
- Leaving the editor in any way saves what was typed.
