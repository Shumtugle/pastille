Android widget · v1.1

Pastille

Six phrases under your thumb. One tap puts a phrase on the clipboard; paste it anywhere.

Tap a pill. It really copies.

What it does

  • A single line of glass on the home screen, four cells wide: four pills and a round edit button. A short label on each pill, the whole phrase behind it.
  • Tap a pill and the phrase is on the clipboard. No window opens; the pill answers by flooding with its own colour for a moment.
  • Each pill keeps its colour by place, so the hand learns where a phrase lives before the eye reads it.
  • An empty pill is a dashed outline. Tap it and the editor opens right there.
  • Every widget on the home screen keeps its own six phrases, and its own glass: dimmed 10, 40, 70 or 90 percent.
  • Leaving the editor in any way saves what was typed.

Any size

Get it

The APK is attached to each release of this repository. Android 8.0 or newer. No network, no permissions, nothing collected. Phrases stay on the phone and are not backed up, so this is not a place for passwords.
